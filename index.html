The problem

Finding the right doctor session on eChannelling takes far too long.

eChannelling is how most of Sri Lanka books doctor appointments online. But its flow makes you open one doctor, on one day, at one hospital at a time — so a simple question like “when can I actually see this specialist near me this week?” turns into a long trail of clicks, back-buttons and re-searches.

1 dayat a time — no single view of a doctor's whole week.
Many tabsto compare doctors, hospitals and times side by side.
Dead endsfull sessions look the same as open ones until you click.
The pains

What makes the current experience frustrating

The information you need to make one decision is scattered across many screens.

No week at a glance

You check availability day by day. Seeing every slot a doctor has this week means paging through the calendar over and over.

Painful to compare

Two doctors, three hospitals, five possible times — comparing them means juggling tabs and holding it all in your head.

Full slots waste clicks

Sold-out sessions sit right next to open ones. You only find out a time is gone after you've clicked into it.

Hard to find a shared time

Need a slot where two doctors are at the same hospital on the same day? There's no easy way to line that up.

Receipts are a scavenger hunt

Pulling up an existing booking or its receipt means digging back through the site with your reference in hand.

Retyping the same details

Name, NIC and phone number, entered again for every booking — for you and for each family member you channel for.

What you get

Everything on one fast screen — then straight to checkout

Doctor Lanka reads the same live eChannelling data, but shows it the way you actually think about it: by doctor, by week, by what's open.

The whole week, instantly

Search a doctor and see every session across the week in one calendar — no more paging one day at a time.

Compare doctors side by side

Add a second or third doctor and see them together — and find the times where they share a hospital and a day.

Hide what's already full

One toggle drops every sold-out session, so what's left on screen is what you can actually book right now.

Look up a booking in a paste

Drop in a reference number or the summary URL and pull up the receipt for a booking you've already made.

Your details, pre-filled

Save name, NIC, mobile and email once and the patient form arrives filled in — for you or whoever you're booking for.

Checkout stays official

Pick your slot here; payment finishes on echannelling.com itself — same trusted checkout, none of the searching.

How it works

Three steps, and you're at the payment page

Doctor Lanka handles the finding and comparing. eChannelling handles the booking and the money — exactly as before.

Search & compare

Open Doctor Lanka, search for your doctor, and read the whole week at once. Add more doctors and toggle off full sessions to narrow it down fast.

Pick your slot

Click the time that works — the open one, at the hospital you want, on the day that suits you. That's the decision made in seconds.

Pay on eChannelling

Doctor Lanka hands you to the official site at the patient-details step with your slot already set and your details filled in. You review and pay as usual.

Payment stays officialMoney only ever changes hands on echannelling.com's own checkout — never inside the extension.
