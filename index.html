Privacy

No account, no server, nothing to collect.

Doctor Lanka runs entirely in your browser. Your patient details — name, NIC, mobile, email — are stored only on your device (Chrome's local storage) and are sent only to the official booking site when you book, exactly as if you'd typed them on the site.

Doctor Lanka is an independent companion tool that provides a faster, more versatile interface — a "wrapper" — over Sri Lanka's public doctor-channelling service. Its purpose is not to support, replace, or abuse any existing service, but to give users a smoother, improved booking experience. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any booking provider, and all appointments and payments are completed on the provider's own official site. Available by invitation only.
