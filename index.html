DocLa (formerly Doctor Lanka) is an independent companion tool that provides a faster interface — a "wrapper" — over Sri Lanka's public doctor-channelling service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any booking provider, and all appointments and payments are completed on the provider's own official site. In limited early release.
