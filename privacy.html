Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

In one line: Doctor Lanka runs entirely in your browser. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your details stay on your device and are sent only to the official eChannelling booking site when you choose to book — exactly as if you had typed them there yourself.

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the Doctor Lanka Chrome extension (the “extension”), published by BSC Extensions. Doctor Lanka is an independent companion tool that provides a faster weekly-calendar view over Sri Lanka's public doctor-channelling service (echannelling.com). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any booking provider.

What data the extension handles

To pre-fill the booking form for you, the extension can store the patient details you enter into it:

  • Patient name
  • NIC (National Identity Card) number
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Your own preferences and settings for the calendar view

You enter these yourself, and only if you want the booking form pre-filled. The extension does not read, collect, or infer any other personal information.

Where your data is stored

Everything above is stored only on your own device, in Chrome's chrome.storage.local. It is never uploaded to us, and we operate no server or database that could receive it. There is no account to create and no sign-in.

What data is shared, and with whom

The extension sends data to one place only: the official eChannelling website and its own public API, and only for the actions you take.

  • Reading availability: the extension calls eChannelling's own public data endpoints (doctors, hospitals, sessions) — the same ones the website itself uses — to build the calendar. No personal data of yours is included in these requests.
  • Booking: when you choose a slot and continue to checkout, your patient details are handed to eChannelling's own booking form — exactly as if you had typed them on the site. Payment and confirmation always complete on eChannelling's own official checkout, under their terms and privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other party, and we run no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Browser permissions we request, and why

  • storage — to keep your patient details and settings on your device.
  • tabs — to open the calendar and hand your chosen slot to your eChannelling tab.
  • declarativeNetRequest — to add the Origin/Referer headers eChannelling's API gateway requires (a page request cannot set these). The rules are static; the extension never reads request or response bodies.
  • Access to echannelling.com and its public API gateway — to read the public session data and seed the slot you pick into the site's checkout.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only in your browser, you are always in control of it. To erase everything the extension has stored, clear its data from within the extension, or remove the extension from chrome://extensions — uninstalling deletes its local storage. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

Doctor Lanka is a general-purpose booking helper and is not directed at children. It collects no data centrally and stores only what an adult enters to book an appointment.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
